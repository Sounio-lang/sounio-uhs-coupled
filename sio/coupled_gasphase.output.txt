== reading ==
   The mesocosm feed is AT stoichiometry, so its co-injected CO2
   covers the H2 it arrives with and calcite changes nothing: the
   ratio is 1 at every gas volume. The field feed carries 13 times
   more H2 than its own CO2 can convert, so carbon runs out first
   and calcite is what relieves it. Lobodice, at 12 % CO2, is
   CO2-rich and calcite is again nearly irrelevant.

   So H1a is NOT universally true, and this test says where it is
   false: at a stoichiometric or CO2-rich feed. It survives F1 only
   for a CO2-POOR feed, which is what the field trial actually was.
