== channels (b), (c), (d): REFUSED ==
   (b) abiotic mineral redox  -- no measured rate in this study

   THIS MODULE REFUSES TO EMIT A TOTAL ABIOTIC BAND.
   Summing (a) alone and calling it the abiotic band would assert
   that (b), (c) and (d) are ZERO. That null has not been measured
   here, and asserting an unmeasured null is the same error as
   inventing a number, with the sign reversed.

== F3 verdict, on what IS measured ==
   At the gas saturation this study uses throughout (Sg = 50 %),
   the one abiotic channel that can be computed exactly accounts
   for under 2 % H2 loss, against roughly 16 % observed. It does
   NOT encompass the field point.

   The site's own sterile control agrees: abiotic loss is 1.22 to
   2.20 %, and killing the microbes removes 5 to 10 times the loss.

   F3 DOES NOT FIRE. The abiotic band does not encompass the field
   point, so the coupling is NOT superfluous on this evidence.
   This is a NEGATIVE result for F3 and therefore a positive one
   for keeping the biotic model -- stated as such, since F3 was
   pre-registered as a chance to discard the whole apparatus.

   CAVEAT, stated rather than buried: dissolution is treated here
   as unrecovered. On drawdown some dissolved H2 exsolves and IS
   recovered, so channel (a) as computed is an UPPER bound on the
   dissolution loss. That makes the F3 verdict STRONGER, not
   weaker: the true abiotic dissolution loss is smaller still.
