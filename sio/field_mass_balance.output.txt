   The brine can hold three times the CO2 that disappeared. The
   CO2 mass-balance residual therefore CANNOT be assigned to
   methanation without first excluding dissolution, and the paper
   does not exclude it. The biotic share above is an UPPER BOUND,
   and the true biotic loss may be far smaller.
