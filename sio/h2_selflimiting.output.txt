== verdict on H2 ==
   H2 predicted self-limitation over PART of parameter space, and
   that is what the sweep shows: there is a calcium threshold, the
   loop closes above it and never closes below it. The hypothesis
   is CONFIRMED IN FORM. Its content is the threshold, which was
   not stated in advance and is reported here as measured.

   Note what this means alongside H1a. H1a needs calcite to
   DISSOLVE and supply carbon; H2 has it PRECIPITATE and remove
   carbon. They are the same mineral running in opposite
   directions, and which one runs is set by the saturation state.
   A reservoir cannot be in both regimes at once.

== consequence for F1, stated carefully ==
   sio/coupled_gasphase.sio holds DIC at a FIXED calcite ceiling in
   its with-calcite branch. That ceiling is not fixed: it falls by
   an order of magnitude as methanogenesis raises pH, and past the
   crossover the mineral is removing carbon rather than supplying
   it. Calcite's own net contribution never exceeds a few per cent
   of the carbon the water already held.

   So F1 = 4.3 to 13 is an UPPER BOUND that a closed system does
   not attain. H1a contains the seed of its own termination: the
   reaction it enables drives the water to the state where the
   mineral stops enabling it.

   ONE IDEALISATION IS DOING WORK HERE AND IS DECLARED. This test
   is CLOSED -- no gas phase resupplies CO2, so nothing buffers the
   pH rise. A gas reservoir rich in CO2 would hold pH down and keep
   the mineral dissolving. But the regime where H1a matters at all
   is the CO2-POOR feed, where that buffer is weakest and depletes
   first. H2 therefore bites hardest exactly where H1a lives, and
   the two pre-registered hypotheses are not independent.

   Quantifying the buffered case is the next increment and is NOT
   claimed here.
