== verdict ==
   The field observation is kinetically IMPOSSIBLE under the
   archive's parameterisation and comfortably FEASIBLE under
   Strobel's, at a cell density BELOW the one actually measured
   in a town-gas storage. The gap was never about how much
   biomass a reservoir holds -- the archive's own upper bound is
   close to the measured field value. It is about the rate
   constant, and the archive's was fitted to a 12 700-year
   trajectory, not to a storage cycle.

   CARRIED AS A BAND, NOT A NUMBER. Every figure above spans the
   full measured 24.6-266.5 fg/cell range, and that range is
   itself an extrapolation from organisms that are not these.
