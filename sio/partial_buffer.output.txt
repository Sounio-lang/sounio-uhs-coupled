== verdict on F1 for the field gas ==
   With H2's loop honoured and the field's own 0.19 % CO2 buffering it,
   removing calcite changes cumulative biotic H2 loss by 1.03 at ordinary
   gas saturation, and by at most 1.21 anywhere in calcium tested. F1's
   bar is a factor of 2. Under this model H1a FAILS F1 at the field too.
   And the maximum net dissolution is reached at zero carbon methanated
   at every gas volume: calcite never responds to methanogenesis by
   dissolving. All the carbon it gives is given before the microbes start.

   Declared, and what would overturn it: equilibrium calcite (kinetics can
   only lower psi_max), a closed vessel, ionic strength held at 0.5, the
   Aux Vases water as proxy for Lehen's, and psi_max counted as available
   in full, which is generous to H1a. An open system with CO2 resupply
   from outside the storage would be a different question.
