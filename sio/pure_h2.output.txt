== VERDICT ==
   Read naively, calcite is worth a factor of tens to hundreds in
   pure hydrogen storage, and the ratio is unbounded in gas volume.
   Honoured against H2, the mineral supplies a few per cent of the
   carbon the formation already held, and F1 collapses to near 1.

   H1a FAILS F1 FOR PURE HYDROGEN STORAGE -- and it fails hardest
   in the regime where the naive reading makes it look strongest.
   The mechanism is largest exactly where it is most completely
   self-limiting, because the CO2 that would buffer the pH rise is
   the same CO2 whose absence made the mechanism look large.

   That is a claim about a closed system with calcite at
   saturation and a fixed calcium. It is NOT a claim that biotic
   H2 loss is small in pure hydrogen storage: the formation's own
   DIC and its sulfate remain available, and by the numbers above
   they alone consume a few per cent of the stored hydrogen.
